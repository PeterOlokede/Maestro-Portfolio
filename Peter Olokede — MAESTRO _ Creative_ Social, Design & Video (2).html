Questions

Before you reach out.

What should I send to get started?

A short description of the project, who it is for, where it will be published, and any deadline. Logos, brand colours and reference examples help, but are not required.

Who do you work with?

Founders, startups, student organisations, community groups and brands that need social media support, design, content or video editing.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. After the brief I give you a timeline, and we agree it before work begins.

How is pricing handled?

I quote per project or per month once I understand the scope. Send your brief and I will respond with a clear proposal.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Revision rounds are agreed upfront so expectations are clear for both sides.
