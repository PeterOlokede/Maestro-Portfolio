Brand notes

What makes a brand look credible online

Short, practical notes you can use today.

Why your brand needs a portfolio website

Instagram is rented space. The algorithm decides who sees you, and a page can lose reach or access overnight. A website is yours. It looks the same to every visitor and keeps working while you sleep. It gives you four things a social page cannot:

  • Credibility. People search a name before they message, and a real site tells them you are serious.
  • Proof in one place. Your best work, your process and your contact details sit together, not buried in old posts.
  • Control. You decide what visitors see first, and an app update never changes it.
  • A place to send people. Every post, bio and WhatsApp reply can point to one link that explains everything.
What the first screen of your website must say

Visitors decide in seconds, so the top of your page has to answer three questions:

  1. Who is this for?
  2. What do you do or sell?
  3. What should I do next?

Answer the first two in one clear sentence and make the third a single button. Then show the page to a stranger for five seconds and ask what you do. If they can't tell you, rewrite the top line.

Instagram or website? You need both

Instagram brings people to you. A website turns their interest into messages and bookings. They do different jobs, so they work best together.

  • Put your website link in your bio, not only a WhatsApp number.
  • Use the same colours, fonts and tone on both, so each one looks like the other.
  • Show your best work on the site too, so a new visitor sees it without scrolling through your feed.

Process

How we work together

  1. Brief

    You tell me the goal, audience, platforms and any brand guidelines. I ask the questions that matter.
